OBJECTIVE 1 — INITIAL PURPOSE AND FINAL GOAL      (docs/project_understanding.md)
================================================================================

INITIAL PURPOSE
  ITS models image generation as a CONTROLLED STOCHASTIC PROCESS, treating sampling
  as a stochastic optimal control problem. A pretrained score model supplies the
  baseline reverse-diffusion drift; ITS augments it with a learned neural control
  drift u_theta(x,t). The central controlled SDE:

      dx = [ f(x,t) - g(t)^2 * grad_x log p_t(x) + g(t)*u_theta(x,t) ] dt + g(t) dW_bar
           |________ pretrained score drift ________|  |__ learned __|

  Control is not free: Girsanov gives its exact information cost,
      KL(P^u || P^0) = E[ int_0^1 ||u(x,t)||^2 / (2 g(t)^2) dt ]

  THE FIVE TRACKED QUANTITIES:
    1. Sample quality        — FID / Inception Score
    2. Control energy        — E[||u||^2]
    3. Entropy production    — non-equilibrium irreversibility of the sampling path
    4. Information cost      — path-space KL via the Girsanov log-RN derivative
    5. Compute cost          — NFE per sample

  The intended payoff: a Pareto frontier over (quality, energy, information,
  compute), supported by a thermodynamic suite (Crooks, Jarzynski, entropy profiles).

FINAL GOAL
  A rigorous, publishable paper whose every claim is supported by scientifically
  sound experimental results. Not "a paper that says ITS wins" — a paper whose
  claims match what the experiments actually show.

  The realistic contribution CANNOT be determined yet. The project believed it was
  "collapse diagnosis + redesigned objective + thermodynamic framework -> TMLR".
  That framing is not currently supported (see Objective 2). The honest restatement:

      A paper demonstrating image generation as a controlled stochastic process with
      full thermodynamic and information-theoretic accounting — ON A SAMPLER THAT
      PROVABLY SAMPLES FROM THE DATA DISTRIBUTION, WITH CONTROL PROVABLY APPLIED,
      AND WITH A PATH-KL THAT PROVABLY IS THE GIRSANOV KL.

  All three "provably"s failed. All three are now fixed in code but unproven on real
  data. Whether the contribution ends up positive, negative, or methodological cannot
  be known until the substrate is validated and the experiments re-run. Any framing
  chosen now would be a guess.

THE FOUR FATAL DEFECTS (each with an executed proof)

 A1. THE SAMPLER DID NOT SAMPLE FROM THE DATA DISTRIBUTION.
     score_sde.py:66 (and duplicated in simulate_path): drift = 0.5*beta*(-x-score)
     with a POSITIVE step while t descends. Sign inverted on both terms, factor 2 on
     the score. PROOF, exact analytic score, target variance 0.25:
         steps:   50      100     200     500
         code:    20.32   20.65   21.00   21.13    <- ~84x too wide, NEVER improves
         fixed:   0.2570  0.2525  0.2511  0.2516   <- converges, improves with NFE
     This exactly predicts the observed SDE FID 326.1/325.2/325.7 at NFE 50/100/200 —
     flat, which is impossible for a working sampler.

 A2. VE-TRAINED, VP-SAMPLED. score_training.py:244 uses the VE kernel
     `noisy = x + sigma*noise` with target `-noise/sigma`; the sampler applied VP
     linear-beta dynamics. Incompatible diffusion families. Also explains why even
     the "good" DDPM baseline is FID 197 — a healthy FashionMNIST model reaches < 20.

 A3. CONTROL WAS SILENTLY DISABLED IN EVERY EVALUATION.  *** THE BIG ONE ***
     ScoreSDEConfig.control_weight defaults to 0.0 and gates the control branch. NO
     eval call-site ever passed it. The policy was loaded, passed in, never applied.
     This explains, exactly:
       - controlled FID 327.47 ~= SDE baseline 325-326  -> the SAME computation
       - ablation_study.json: B/C/D/F/conv/no-EMA all fid=326.0024719238281 to 16
         digits, ACROSS DIFFERENT ARCHITECTURES
       - conv_checkpoint_fid_trajectory.json: all 10 epochs fid=335.1768493652344 —
         an exactly constant "learning curve"
       - controlled_config_d_multiseed.json BYTE-IDENTICAL to controlled_conv_multiseed.json

     CONSEQUENCE: the inference "controlled FID = baseline FID => the controller
     collapsed" is INVALID. The project's headline finding loses its FID evidence.
     The collapse RATIO (0.061%) is a real training-diagnostic measurement — but of a
     run conducted inside the A1 dynamics.

 A4. THE GIRSANOV / PATH-KL FORMULA WAS WRONG by a factor of beta_t (4 copies).
     Correct: log_rn = <delta,z>/sqrt(var) + ||delta||^2/(2*var); path_kl = +mean(log_rn).
     PROOF: correct formula matches exact Gaussian log-densities to 3.3e-16; the
     code's is off by 0.94 in a single step. Since beta sweeps 0.1->10 this is a
     TIME-VARYING distortion — not absorbable into path_kl_weight. Path-space KL is
     one of the five headline quantities: every path-KL ever reported is wrong.

SESSION 10 v2 OBJECTIVE — STATUS: IT DID NOT STALL, IT DIVERGED
  Run 5A: 39/100 epochs, 176.1 wall-clock HOURS, NEVER EVALUATED.

    cos_sim(score, control) ~= -0.1: the control is orthogonal noise, not steering
  So the controller went from COLLAPSED (0.061%) to EXPLODED (55,000%). Neither works.

  WHY, in two phases (adversarial verification corrected my initial single-cause account):
    Phase 1 (ep1-5): WarmupSchedule zeroes path_kl_weight AND reinforce_weight while
      detach_control_energy=True removes control energy from the gradient -> ||u|| had
      NO REGULARISER AT ALL. Control energy grew 25,000x before any Girsanov term was on.
    Phase 2 (ep6+): the REINFORCE surrogate is UNBOUNDED BELOW. total_loss goes negative
      at exactly ep6, when reinforce_weight first becomes non-zero.
  grad_clip=1.0 WAS ACTIVE and could not help: clipping bounds step SIZE, not
  divergence. On an objective unbounded below, clipped descent still marches to
  infinity — at a bounded rate. That is precisely the observed steady monotonic growth.
