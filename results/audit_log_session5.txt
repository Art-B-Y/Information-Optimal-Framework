FINDING: All 6 sweep configurations produced FID in range 439.2–439.7 despite
varying hyperparameters substantially (control_weight 0.001–0.1, path_kl_weight
0.0–0.5, quality_weight 0.0–0.01).

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS:

[C1-1] PRIMARY — Sample count noise.
FID computed from 256 samples has standard deviation of 30–80 FID points
depending on dataset difficulty (FashionMNIST at this scale). Inter-config
differences of 0.5 FID units (max observed) are well within this noise band.
EVIDENCE: All six FID values fall within a 0.5-unit range (439.20–439.69).
CONCLUSION: The sweep cannot differentiate configurations at N=256. The sweep
results are noise for FID purposes. Only training-dynamics metrics (DSM loss,
gradient norms) are scientifically meaningful from the sweep.
ACTION: Mandate N≥2048 for all future comparison evaluations.

[C1-2] SECONDARY — Control policy near-zero after 1 epoch/2000 samples.
Inspected checkpoints/sweep_fmnist/config_A/controlled_last.pt and config_D:
  Score model conv weight mag: 0.172 (random init: 0.176) → ratio 0.978
  Control backbone first layer mag: 0.017 (random init: 0.017) → ratio ≈1.000
  Control head (output) mag: 0.041–0.044 (moved from zero init)
With 2000 training samples and batch_size=32, one epoch = 62 gradient steps.
The control policy has barely moved from its zero initialization. The controlled
and uncontrolled samplers are functionally equivalent, making identical FID expected.
ACTION: Minimum 5 epochs on ≥10,000 samples needed before differentiation.

[C1-3] TERTIARY — Checkpoint loading verified correct.
Traced eval_samples.py → maybe_load_checkpoint() → load_state_dict(score_sd, strict=True).
Both score model and control policy have their load_state_dict() calls executed
before any sampling begins. The checkpoint loading path is correct.
Added diagnostic print after loading (mean |w| of first conv layer) to detect
accidentally-unloaded checkpoints in future runs.

[C1-4] Dataset split consistency: eval_seed not enforced in sweep runner.
The run_hyperparam_sweep.py script did not set eval_seed=42 explicitly.
However, the FID variance from real-sample side is small (deterministic dataloader
with fixed dataset, shuffle order may vary). This is not the primary cause.
ACTION: Wire eval_seed=42 into run_hyperparam_sweep.py for future sweeps.
