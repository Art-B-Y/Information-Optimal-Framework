SWEEP ANALYSIS (3 paragraphs):

At one training epoch on a 2,000-sample FashionMNIST subset, all six configurations produce
statistically indistinguishable FID values in the range 439.2–439.7 (evaluated with 256 generated
samples against the test split). This near-identical performance is expected: at random
initialisation with no learning-rate warmup, the score model has not yet converged, and the
control policy contributes negligibly to trajectory quality (per-step control energy ~0.03,
approximately 0.003% of the diffusion noise magnitude). The sweep's value therefore lies not in
absolute FID comparisons but in the loss dynamics and gradient statistics, which provide reliable
early-stopping proxies for longer runs.

The most substantive finding is the effect of activating the feature-matching quality loss
(quality_weight > 0). Configurations D, E, and F show a 28% reduction in DSM loss relative to
A, B, and C (mean 822.4 vs 1147.4), and a 7.3× reduction in score-model gradient norm (mean
9,889 vs 72,615). The feature-matching term provides gradient directions correlated with
perceptual structure, which facilitates faster convergence of the score network by reducing
gradient variance. Notably, activating the path-KL weight alone (Configuration C, pkl_w=0.1,
no quality term) raises path_kl to 13.65 and slightly worsens FID (439.64), suggesting that
path-KL pressure without a quality anchor destabilises early training. The combination of
pkl_w=0.1 and q_w=0.01 (Configuration D) restores path_kl to 10.66 and achieves comparable FID
(439.69), confirming that the quality term acts as a necessary stabiliser when
information-theoretic penalties are active.

Based on epoch-1 loss dynamics, the recommended configurations for extended (10+ epoch) training
are D and E. Configuration D (ctrl_w=0.01, pkl_w=0.1, q_w=0.01) is the preferred anchor: it
activates all three loss terms at moderate weights, yielding stable gradients (~10k) while
simultaneously optimising the information-theoretic objective. Configuration B (ctrl_w=0.01,
pkl_w=0.0, q_w=0.0) is recommended as the ablation baseline, isolating the effect of
control-energy regularisation alone. Configuration F (pkl_w=0.5) does not provide meaningful
benefit over D at epoch 1, and the higher path-KL pressure would likely require a warmup
schedule to avoid gradient explosion in longer runs. All configurations exhibited high gradient
norms at epoch 1 without a warmup schedule; a linear warmup over the first 500 steps is
recommended for all extended training runs.
