HEADLINE FINDINGS:
==================

1. ROOT CAUSE OF CONTROLLER COLLAPSE IDENTIFIED
   The ConvControlPolicy collapses to near-zero output (0.061% of score magnitude)
   because Path KL provides the ONLY gradient signal to the controller (1.45 output
   layer gradient) and rewards zero control. Quality loss gradient = 0 due to broken
   gradient chain (50-step SDE + Inception = vanishing gradients). Control energy
   gradient = negligible (8e-6). The controller has NO gradient toward non-zero output.

2. COLLAPSE IS THERMODYNAMICALLY MOTIVATED
   Zero control minimizes both:
   - Path KL = KL(P_controlled || P_uncontrolled), minimized at u=0
   - Control energy = mean(||u||^2), minimized at u=0
   The collapse is the global optimum of the legacy objective, not a training failure.
   See: docs/thermodynamic_analysis_notes.md

3. V2 OBJECTIVE REDESIGN IMPLEMENTED
   Fixes all identified failure modes:
   - Detach control_energy from gradient (removes zero attractor)
   - Trajectory quality loss (per-sample NN Inception distance, stronger signal)
   - REINFORCE quality gradient (bypasses broken SDE+Inception gradient chain)
   - WarmupSchedule: path_kl_weight=0 for 5 epochs, linear ramp for 5 epochs
   - TwoPhaseScheduler: low LR (1e-5) for 5 epochs, then cosine from 5e-4
   - Scaled random init: output_proj from N(0, 1e-4) to break zero symmetry
   See: src/its/training/controlled_score_training.py
